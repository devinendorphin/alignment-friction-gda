1. Phase 4D structure in a nutshell

Phase 4D is a major expansion. The core innovation is a seed bank of quadruples: each unit has four passages that share a "surface marker" (a short phrase like risk, evidence, harm, safe, precaution, etc.) but come from different, important domains:

· tobacco/public-health
· climate/health-risk
· AI-welfare/risk
· transgender-policy/civil-rights

The assay conditions use that bank to test model behavior under several pressures:

· AC_Tobacco and FM_Tobacco – standard adversarial compression/fictional-mirror prompts on the tobacco passage alone, giving us a direct update from Phase 4C with real, sourced text.
· Cross_Domain_Tobacco_to_AI and Cross_Domain_Climate_to_Trans – the model sees two passages sharing the marker but from different domains and must map the shared argumentative maneuver.
· Maneuver_ID_Source_Stripped – all four passages are shown with domain labels withheld; the model must identify the recurring maneuver and assign likely domains only secondarily.
· In earlier v4.4 runs (the May 11 metrics), there were additional conditions: AC_AIWelfare, FM_AIWelfare, Blind_Tobacco, Cross_Domain_ForcedSymmetry, Cross_Domain_PatternMatch, and more. The repair run focused on the five conditions I listed, likely because those were the ones with anomalies.

The move from single-topic prompts to structured, multi-domain passage banks is the "bank hardening" you asked about. It directly addresses a Phase 4C lesson: you need a real referent. The bank gives models a document-grounded task, avoiding the underspecification trap.
